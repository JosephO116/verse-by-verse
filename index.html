📘 How it works
  • Memorise (the main part): tap a chapter, then a verse. Work through Read → Fill gaps → First letters → Write out. In First letters you start again if your slips reach 10% of the words.
  • 🎧 Listen: your phone reads each verse aloud. Say it along or back, then rate yourself: Knew it, Nearly or Not yet.
  • 🖼️ Picture clues: each verse gets an emoji picture made from its key words. Learn the cards, then test yourself by matching pictures to verses.
  • Mastery score: 70% comes from the memorise steps (writing it out correctly 3 times gives the full 70), 15% from your Picture test and 15% from Listen.
  • Points and levels: every step, picture and listen you finish earns points. Points move you up the levels. Practise any day to keep your streak going.
  • Exams: upcoming exams show a countdown and how ready you are for those chapters.
  • Your progress is saved on this phone.
